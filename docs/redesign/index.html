A perennial scapose herb with a basal leaf rosette, typically 5–20 cm tall. Native through most of Europe to North Africa and Madeira, and east to the Caucasus and western and Central Asia. Introduced in Finland, Iceland, the Azores, the Russian Far East, Korea, the Indian Subcontinent, Australia, New Zealand, Hawaii and widely in the Americas.

Flower

Many white ray florets, often pink-tinged at the tips, with ligules usually 4–8 mm long. Disc florets yellow, tubular, about 1.5–2 mm. Involucral bracts in two rows of almost equal length.
